🚧 Road Accident Data Analysis – Task 5 | Prodigy Infotech Internship
This project focuses on analyzing road accident data to uncover key patterns based on road types, surface conditions, weather, and time of day. The goal is to identify accident hotspots and contributing factors, and to propose practical suggestions to improve road safety.

📊 Key Insights:
Single Carriageways accounted for the most accidents (74.88%)

Dry roads were involved in 67.85% of accidents

Urban areas saw more accidents (64.5%) compared to rural areas (35.5%)

73.8% of accidents occurred during daylight

Most frequent vehicles in accidents: Cars, Motorcycles, Buses

Top 10 accident-prone districts include: Birmingham, Leeds, Manchester, Bradford, and more

💡 Suggestions for Road Safety:
Reduce speed limits on single carriageways

Use more signage reminding drivers to check tyre condition

Increase traffic control (signals & police) in urban hotspots

Promote road safety education

Encourage regular use of seat belts and helmets
